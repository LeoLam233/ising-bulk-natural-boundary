# Source provenance and historical limits

The source checkpoint is v38 (SHA-256 `a2142752ec09870431c8e30e96df7710dc11f97067b31af2c71efe6bdb6ee84c`). Its original manifest is `a20903b810fd21424886fd6121077389716032359431031ae62d001e4b35ce66`.

The receiving workflow checked ZIP CRC, all **192 members** against that manifest and the local extraction against the archive. It scanned **189 text files** (157 Markdown, 32 Python) for explicit URLs/source mentions. Three `.pyc` files were not source-scanned. The inventory contains 32 distinct normalized URLs; no explicit GitHub, Zenodo or ResearchGate research URL was found in that text scan. This is an inventory result, not a reconstruction of every past access.

The formal mathematical inputs are mapped in [EXTERNAL_PREMISES.md](EXTERNAL_PREMISES.md). The Melbourne-hosted research PDF in the history was identified as a mirror of Boukraa et al., with a canonical academic counterpart in the reconstruction record. Background lecture notes or problem-selection sources do not silently become premises of the final proof. Project AI notebooks, repairs and reconstructions are internally generated claims to inspect, not trusted mathematical inputs.

The original research access log is incomplete. URL scans and a clean dependency list cannot prove zero unrecorded exposure, zero prior training familiarity or complete isolation. No identified evidence here shows an outside AI proof being assumed as a theorem. It would be unjustified to strengthen that bounded finding into “the entire history is certified uncontaminated.”

Source cleanliness is assessed by actual content and use, not by whether the source author is employed as a mathematician or physicist. A verified informal result could be legitimate mathematics; an AI-generated statement could still be wrong after many approvals. Any historical influence, if identified, must be attributed separately from formal premise use.

Public historical views are generated from frozen project records with documented path/reference redactions. See [VIEW_MANIFEST.json](VIEW_MANIFEST.json), the [frozen archive index](FROZEN_INPUTS.json), and the [scope map](source_coverage_v2.md). They are not relabelled as untouched originals. The original files and receipts remain in the local evidence archive. Third-party reference PDFs and source page images are omitted from the public package.
