Review date: 28 September 2026. Scope: the low-temperature exterior germ of the full, isotropic square-lattice zero-field susceptibility. This is a dated literature-positioning exercise, not comprehensive priority clearance.

The primary comparisons and search families are recorded in [SEARCH_LOG.json](SEARCH_LOG.json). Revision 8 and rc1 had eleven references. Rc2 retains those entries and adds Orrick et al. after the targeted [primary-source check](ORRICK_2001_VERIFICATION.md); its new access record is [SEARCH_LOG_RC2.json](SEARCH_LOG_RC2.json). The earlier search log is preserved unchanged. A reference count does not measure completeness.

Recent superficially similar titles were not treated as equivalent: [Liu 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06011) concerns a finite-volume critical Ising chain one-point function continued in system length. The observable, model and complex variable differ from this manuscript's susceptibility problem. That scope distinction is based on the abstract; it is not a full evaluation of that paper.

Historical influence and final proof dependence are different questions. The original research ledger already mentioned diagonal precedent; earlier reconstructed drafts nevertheless under-cited it. Correcting attribution does not erase that omission. If an informal or AI-assisted source influenced the discovery, it deserves historical attribution even when no result from it is retained as a formal premise. An author's professional status alone determines neither truth nor priority.

The searches, bibliographic metadata and close reading did not reveal an external AI proof imported as an accepted theorem. They cannot certify all historical exposure. See [SOURCE_PROVENANCE.md](SOURCE_PROVENANCE.md).
