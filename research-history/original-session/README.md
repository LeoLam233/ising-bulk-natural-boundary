## What it establishes—and what it does not

The versioned artifacts permit comparisons of saved files and revisions; they do **not** reconstruct every transient attempt, each model call, or a complete chat transcript. The retrospective is a reconstruction, not a contemporaneous stenographic log. All `v1` material predates the first preserved snapshot; therefore an item first observed in v1 cannot be timed more finely.

Its own final label, `PROVISIONAL CENTRAL CLOSURE`, was an initial-session **self-assessment** that a complete candidate chain had been assembled, not a certification of the bulk natural-boundary theorem. Later version-specific [AI adversarial audits](../../audits/README.md), [manuscript repairs](../../provenance/W1_REPAIR_AUDIT.md) and [Lean formalization](../../lean/README.md) must be read separately.
