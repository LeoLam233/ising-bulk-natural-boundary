# Initial-session checkpoints v1–v38

The original Codex session preserved 38 ZIP snapshots. The following is a **milestone** timeline, not timestamps, model-invocation counts or independent full proofs. An item present in v1 may have been created before the first snapshot. The authoritative per-version additions, revisions, contents and 157-note index are in the [original retrospective, Appendices D–E](original-session/ising_project_complete_history_2026-10-10.md).

| Checkpoints | Recorded milestone | Important limitation |
|---|---|---|
| v1–v2 | Target commit, four-particle test, Pfaffian/residue and weighted L2 tail; first prime family, mixed branch | Very-high-order control only; original contour connectors not yet closed |
